Frequently asked questions

Practical answers about Swiss Alpine Routes

Is the app free?

The current 2.3.0 release information describes a free application with no subscriptions, in-app purchases, advertising or account requirement. Future pricing may change.

Which languages are available?

English is the default for new installations. Switch to Türkçe or System Language in Settings → Language. İsviçre Rotam is the Turkish localization of Swiss Alpine Routes.

Does offline navigation work?

Within downloaded map areas, you can view maps, follow saved routes and record GPS tracks. New route calculations, live weather, avalanche reports, closures and transport timetables need an internet connection.

Are SAC ratings official?

The route difficulty tags shown by the app come from OpenStreetMap community data, not official SAC certification. Unknown segments are explicitly shown and may be harder.

Does SOS call rescuers automatically?

No. It displays Swiss emergency numbers and coordinates; tapping a number opens your phone dialer. You must place the call yourself.

Where is my information stored?

Saved routes, GPS recordings and preferences remain on your device. External requests for maps, route planning, weather and transport necessarily transmit relevant query data to their data services. See the privacy policy.
