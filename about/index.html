About & data sources

Independent Swiss Alpine navigation and travel information

Swiss Alpine Routes

An independent iPhone and iPad app by Bülent İmat, previously localized in Turkish under the name İsviçre Rotam. Its English interface is now the default; Turkish is available in Settings.

What the app does

View Swiss maps and official route layers, explore a curated Swiss Top 100, plan hiking, cycling and MTB routes, use GPS and offline map areas, check mountain weather and hazards, or plan day trips using public transport.

Data sources

swisstopo / geo.admin.ch: maps, SWISSIMAGE and swissALTI3D. SchweizMobil: official route layers and stages. MeteoSwiss models via Open-Meteo: mountain weather. SLF: avalanche bulletin. FOEN/BAFU and cantonal sources: warnings. OpenStreetMap contributors: community routing information and difficulty tags. transport.opendata.ch: Swiss transit timetable. Wikimedia Commons: editorial photos with individual credits.

Independence

Swiss Alpine Routes is not affiliated with or endorsed by swisstopo, SchweizMobil, MeteoSwiss, SLF, SBB or any public agency. Map or route display is not confirmation of safety or accessibility.
