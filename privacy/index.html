Privacy policy

How Swiss Alpine Routes uses device and network information

Controller

Independent developer Bülent İmat. Privacy questions: info@isvicrerotam.com. This policy covers Swiss Alpine Routes 2.3.0 (Turkish localization: İsviçre Rotam) and this website.

No account or advertising

The app has no account system, advertising, analytics, tracking SDK, subscription or in-app purchase. It does not require you to identify yourself to use it.

On-device records

Saved plans, imported routes, recorded GPS tracks, preferences, cached data and offline map areas are stored locally on your device. They may be covered by iOS backup settings. You can delete local app data in Settings.

Location permission

Location permission is optional for browsing and manual route planning. During GPS recording that you choose to start, background location access may be used if enabled. The app shows coordinates in the SOS panel when permission is available; it does not alert rescue services.

External data requests

Viewing maps and searching places sends map-area or search requests to swisstopo services. Routing can send selected coordinates to Valhalla/FOSSGIS or BRouter. Route elevation uses swissALTI3D. Avalanche and hazard data are retrieved from SLF and geo.admin.ch sources. Swiss transit queries use transport.opendata.ch. Such services receive the queries needed to answer the request and may process network metadata under their own policies.

Weather proxy for version 2.3.0

Mountain-model weather uses the app-operated server at isvicre-rotam.expo.app, which relays requests to Open-Meteo. The supplied app review notes say only coordinates rounded to approximately 100 metres, elevation and forecast days are sent, the proxy does not store these requests, and responses are cached for 15 minutes. This replaces the outdated statement that the developer operates no server.

Sharing and retention

GPX export and route sharing occur only when initiated by the user. GPS recordings remain local unless you choose to share them. Email messages sent to the support addresses are processed as necessary to respond.

Website

This static information site uses no login, analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. Hosting and external image providers may receive standard network requests when pages are accessed.

Safety and independence

Swiss Alpine Routes is independent, not an official agency product. It does not dispatch rescue teams. Weather-model indications are not official warnings.
