BUILT FOR THE SWISS ALPS

Features

MAPS

Discover the route ahead

Detailed swisstopo cartography, SWISSIMAGE imagery and Swiss hiking and cycling networks help you orient yourself.

  • Explore official map and aerial layers
  • Search places and inspect routes on a real map
  • Keep source information visible and clear

SWISS TOP 100

100 places worth getting out for

Browse a thoughtfully curated Swiss Top 100, from alpine lakes to panoramic viewpoints, with practical details for your visit.

  • Choose a destination by category or interest
  • See highlights, access information and available webcam links
  • Check suitability and conditions rather than relying on guesses

ROUTE PLANNING

Plan the route, see the climb

Create routes across Swiss trail networks, check distance, ascent and descent, and inspect an elevation profile built from swissALTI3D.

  • Route profiles, estimated durations and elevation
  • GPX and GPS tools for outdoor preparation
  • Hiking, cycling and MTB options

HIKING · CYCLING · MTB

Hike farther. Ride smarter.

Choose a hiking trail or follow national and regional cycling and MTB stages, with difficulty and route information where available.

  • SAC hiking difficulty where sourced
  • National and regional cycling and mountain-bike routes
  • Unknown data stays explicitly marked as unknown

Inside Swiss Alpine Routes

Ten original feature screens from Swiss Alpine Routes, including map tools, route planning, offline functions and mountain safety.

The mountain changes. Stay informed.

Weather, avalanche information, trail closures and transport plans are decision aids, not guarantees of safety.

  • Mountain forecasts, SLF avalanche reports and official closures may be delayed or incomplete.
  • SOS information includes Swiss emergency numbers and coordinates; your phone must have coverage to place a call.
  • Always consult current official sources and assess conditions on site.
