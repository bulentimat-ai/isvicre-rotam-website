WEATHER · SLF · SOS

Mountain information when it matters.

Elevation-aware weather forecasts, the SLF avalanche bulletin, Swiss trail closures and emergency numbers support preparation in changing alpine conditions.

  • Elevation-aware MeteoSwiss model forecasts via Open-Meteo
  • SLF avalanche bulletin and official trail closures
  • 112, 144 and Rega 1414, plus coordinates
  • Public transport day trips, outbound and return
This independent app does not contact rescue services, guarantee safety or replace official warnings. Some functions need an internet connection.
