Terms of use

Use Swiss Alpine Routes responsibly

Purpose

Swiss Alpine Routes provides general geographic, travel and outdoor information for Switzerland. It is not an official navigation, rescue or emergency-alert service.

Safety

Route times, difficulty tags, elevation data, weather forecasts, trail closures and avalanche information may be missing, delayed or wrong. Always consult the current official reports, signs and local conditions before visiting the mountains. The app does not guarantee any route is safe.

Third-party data

Maps, routes, forecasts, conditions and transport data originate from public or open third-party services, which can change or become temporarily unavailable. Attribution is provided in the app and the About page.

Privacy

See our privacy policy for information about local records, location access and requests made to third-party data services.

Support

For questions about use or limitations, visit the support or contact pages.
