Support

Help with Swiss Alpine Routes

Technical support

For app issues and feedback, contact destek@isvicrerotam.com. Include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, app version, steps to reproduce and the observed result. Do not send private GPS traces or exact locations to public issue trackers.

Emergency notice

The app does not contact emergency services. In Switzerland call 112 for emergencies; Rega 1414 for air rescue. Tapping emergency numbers in the app opens the phone dialer; you make the call.
